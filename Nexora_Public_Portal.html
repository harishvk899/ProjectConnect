Project Connect — Community Rules

Use your own identity and accurate account details. Respect other members, get consent before sharing contact details or media, and do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, or share another person's private information.

Keep personal information private. Share your location only when you understand who can see it. Report suspicious behavior and block members who make you uncomfortable.

Project Connect is being released in stages. Some services, including social discovery, groups, push notifications, media sharing, and account safety tools, may not yet be available. These rules are an interim notice and do not replace the final Terms of Service and Privacy Policy that must be published before a full public launch.
