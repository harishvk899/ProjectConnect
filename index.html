Project Connect — Preview Terms

Use an accurate date of birth and do not impersonate another person. Respect other members, get consent before sharing contact details, and do not harass, threaten, spam, or share another person's private information.

Student Mode is intended to stay separate from adult-oriented features, and AI features are not part of Student Mode. Location features, when introduced, should be opt-in and should not expose a precise location by default.

This is a prototype. Sign-in, messages, profile visibility, blocking, read receipts, and other actions are demonstrations only. No real identity is verified, no real messages are delivered, and these sample terms are not a final legal agreement. The production version needs properly reviewed terms and privacy notices.
